How this is worked out: each service row has two figures — the Bank amount (government/bank fee the customer pays, which you pass on) and the Service charge (what you keep for doing the work). Profit = total service charge − expense; the bank column never enters the profit, since it isn't your money.

The cash section is a separate check on your till. Cash should build up as opening + everything collected from customers (bank + service charge) − what you paid out to the bank/portal − expenses. That should equal your actual closing count — if it doesn't, the note above tells you how much cash is short or over for the day.
